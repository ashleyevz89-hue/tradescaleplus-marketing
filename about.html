Why it is built this way

Built to answer from your site and check itself.

Most chat widgets are a form with a personality. Ours was designed around what a buyer actually asks, and what a good salesperson would never do.

  • It answers only from your own website and data, and every reply is checked in code before it is shown.
  • It never invents a figure. Not a price, not a monthly payment, not an APR. If it does not know, it says so and offers to get somebody who does.
  • On a car dealer’s site it never offers a car that has gone. Sold and reserved stock drops out of its answers at the next daily refresh.
  • It asks for the booking or the enquiry, the way a salesperson would, rather than waiting to be asked.
  • It tells the customer where their details are going before it takes them.

That is not caution for its own sake. A business cannot put something on its own website that might quote a price or a rate it made up, and no amount of clever answering is worth that risk.
