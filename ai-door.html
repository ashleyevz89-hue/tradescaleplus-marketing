What goes wrong today

A page people can see
can be blank to an AI

Some pages are built by scripts that run in your visitor's browser. The listings appear when the scripts run. An AI assistant that does not run those scripts reads an empty shell. That is what we mean by a page ChatGPT cannot normally read.

Questions, answered

The AI door, answered.

What is the AI door?

It is how our two install lines let an AI assistant that reads the web find your live listings. On our test page it led ChatGPT to the live stock on its own, with no setup by the user.

What did you test?

A test page whose listings are drawn by JavaScript, which ChatGPT cannot normally read. We asked ChatGPT "I need a 7 seater under 18,000" and it answered with all five correct cars and prices. A follow-up about electric cars returned all three correct. We saw it in our server logs on 1 November 2026.

Does it only work with ChatGPT?

Claude's reader runs the page and loads the agent itself. It is plain web pages, so any AI that reads the web can use it. There is also an MCP connector for AI apps that take connectors.

Will ChatGPT recommend my business?

No one can promise that. AI assistants choose their own answers. The door gives an AI that reads the web a way to find your live listings.

Do I have to do anything?

Add the two lines of code: a link tag and a script. Nothing else on your site changes.
