Why it matters

AI can only recommend
what it can read.

More people now ask an AI assistant before they search: “who’s a good garage near me?”, “which local dealer has an automatic SUV under £15k?”. The assistant answers from the websites it can read and trust.

Most websites were built for people, not machines. Words that appear only after scripts run, business details that are not in structured data, robots rules that shut crawlers out: each one makes you harder to read, and easier to leave out.

The score shows you where you stand in a few seconds, in plain English, with exactly what to fix.
