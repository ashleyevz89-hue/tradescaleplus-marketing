What AI sees today

Most dealer websites
are hard for AI to read.

Car pages are built for people: photos, sliders, finance boxes and banners. An AI reading one has to guess which number is the price, which is the monthly figure, and whether the car is still for sale. When it cannot be sure, it leaves your car out, or sends the buyer to a listing site instead of to you.

What changes with TradeScale Plus

Three ways in.
All from your own stock.

Car data on every car page. Each of your car pages carries clean, structured car data in the schema.org format that search engines and AI read: make, model, year, mileage, price and whether it is available, taken from your live stock.

A stock connector for AI apps. Your stock gets its own connector, built on MCP, the standard AI apps such as Claude and ChatGPT use to plug into live data. Through it an AI assistant can search your cars, check one is still available, ask about it and book a test drive, every answer coming from your own stock.

Ready for AI agents on your pages. When an AI agent browses your website for a buyer, the assistant hands it the same tools right there on the page, so it does not have to guess from the layout.

Honest by design

Only what
you publish.

Everything an AI gets comes from your own stock and moves with it: a sold car drops out, a reserved car is never offered as available, and nothing is estimated or invented. Finance is only ever your own published figures. A test drive booked through an AI arrives with you like any other enquiry, with the car and the buyer’s details.

Questions, answered

AI search, answered.

Will ChatGPT definitely recommend my cars?

Nobody can promise what an AI will recommend. What this does is make sure that when an AI looks, it finds your real cars, correctly priced and available, instead of guessing or using somebody else’s listing.

What do I have to do?

Nothing beyond the install. The same two lines of code that add the website sales admin add the car data and the connector, and we do the install for you.

Does it share anything private?

No. It only uses what your website already shows the public: your cars, prices and published details. Buyers’ details are never part of it.

Does this replace my listings on the big car sites?

No, it works alongside them. It makes your own website, where you pay no listing fee and the enquiry is yours, readable by AI too.
