How it works

Three steps, no phone call.

01

The buyer picks a day and a time

The next five days you’re open, a time every hour inside your opening hours.

02

You press Confirm

You get an email with the day, the time, the car and the buyer’s number. Can’t do it? Reply and it goes straight to the buyer, and agree another.

03

The buyer hears back

A buyer who gave their email gets their confirmation straight away, and a reminder the day before.

Day and night

Your showroom is closed. Your website isn’t.

At 9:47pm it still answers, and books the test drive for when you’re open. Leads that come in while you’re shut are counted apart in your dashboard, so you see what the evenings are worth.

Questions

Test drives, answered.

How do test drives work?

The buyer picks one of the next five days you are open and a time inside your opening hours. You get an email to confirm it. Once you press Confirm, a buyer who gave their email gets their confirmation straight away and a reminder the day before.

What stops it booking when we’re closed?

Only the days you are open and times inside your opening hours are offered.

What if I can’t do the time?

Reply to the email and it goes straight to the buyer, and agree another.

Where do I see my bookings?

Each one is emailed to you the moment it is made, and your dashboard keeps a diary of test drives and viewings to confirm and record.

Is it for estate agents too?

Yes. On every listing the panel takes a viewing request, booked for the listing’s consultant.
