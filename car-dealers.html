Questions

For car dealers, answered.

Whose finance rate does it use?

Yours. It works out the buyer's monthly from your own lender's terms, to the penny, and shows your representative example with it. Where a figure cannot be exact, it says so and offers the nearest it can.

Does it value part-exchanges?

No. It takes the buyer's registration and mileage and sends them to your team. No price is given.

How do test drives work?

The buyer picks one of the next five days you are open and a time inside your opening hours. You get an email to confirm it. Once you press Confirm, a buyer who gave their email gets their confirmation straight away and a reminder the day before.

What does it answer from?

Only your own stock and pages: each car's own listing, your opening hours, your finance and what your website says. Every reply is checked in code before a buyer sees it. When only your team can answer, it says so and takes the buyer's details.

Do I need a new website?

No. Two lines of code go on the website you already have, added by your web company or through your own Google Tag Manager. We set it up and run it.

What does it cost?

It is priced for your business: your stock size, how many websites or branches it covers and the features you use. Get a quote
