How it installs

Two lines of code

A link tag and a script. Nothing else on your site changes.

Your two lines
<link rel="mcp" href="https://demo.tradescaleplus.com/connector/YOUR-SLUG"><noscript><a href="https://demo.tradescaleplus.com/ai/YOUR-SLUG">Our live listings, with prices and a search, readable without JavaScript</a></noscript><script src="https://demo.tradescaleplus.com/embed.js?slug=YOUR-SLUG" data-slug="YOUR-SLUG" async></script>

YOUR-SLUG stands for your own site's name.

Questions, answered

The Car Agent, answered.

How does the agent know my cars?

It answers from your own website and data. Your stock is refreshed daily from your own site.

Whose finance rate does it use?

Yours. It quotes finance at your own published rate.

How do bookings work?

The agent books viewings and test drives within your opening hours. You confirm each booking by email.

What stops it making things up?

It answers only from your own website and data, and every reply is checked in code before it is shown. Invented prices, invented cars, wrong links and false claims that something is free are caught and corrected.

What do I get as the dealer?

Enquiries by email, hot-lead alerts, a dashboard of every conversation and of what visitors asked for that was not in stock, and a Monday report.

How much does it cost?

Every agent is priced for your business. The price depends on your stock size, how many websites or branches the agent covers and the features you use. Get a quote, and you can start with a free demo built on your own stock.
