How it installs

Two lines of code

A link tag and a script. Nothing else on your site changes.

Your two lines
<link rel="mcp" href="https://demo.tradescaleplus.com/connector/YOUR-SLUG"><noscript><a href="https://demo.tradescaleplus.com/ai/YOUR-SLUG">Our live listings, with prices and a search, readable without JavaScript</a></noscript><script src="https://demo.tradescaleplus.com/embed.js?slug=YOUR-SLUG" data-slug="YOUR-SLUG" async></script>

YOUR-SLUG stands for your own site's name.

Questions, answered

Custom agents, answered.

What kinds of website can have an agent?

Car dealers and property agencies first. We can also work on any other website, such as courses, events, services and catalogues.

What will the agent do on my site?

It answers visitors from your own website and data, takes them to the right page, filters a list page, compares two or three things side by side, saves favourites, and keeps their journey. It books, or takes the enquiry.

How does it get onto my website?

With two lines of code: a link tag and a script. Nothing else on your site changes.

Can it make things up?

Every reply is checked in code before it is shown. Invented prices, wrong links and false claims that something is free are caught and corrected. It answers only from your own website and data.

What do I get from it?

Enquiries by email, hot-lead alerts, a dashboard of every conversation and of what visitors asked for that was not there, and a Monday report.
