Questions, answered

The assistant, answered.

What is the TradeScale Plus website sales admin?

TradeScale Plus is a website sales admin for UK used-car dealers: a chat assistant that lives on your website, knows your entire live stock car by car, takes buyers straight to the right car, books real viewings and test drives, quotes finance at your own representative APR, and hands you qualified leads.

How does it know my stock?

It reads your actual live stock from your website, make, model, year, price, mileage, colour, fuel, gearbox, spec, features and MOT for every car, and relearns it every day. It never offers a sold or reserved car.

Is this just another chatbot?

No. A chatbot answers off a FAQ and has no idea what's on your forecourt. This is a salesperson: it answers from your real stock, navigates buyers to the exact car's page, books real appointments inside your opening hours, and quotes finance on the actual car. See our full comparison with live chat, chatbots and lead forms.

Is TradeScale Plus an AI live chat for car dealers?

Yes, and it goes further than live chat. It sits on your own website like live chat does, but where live chat takes a message for someone to answer later, it answers there and then from your live stock: the car, the price, the mileage, the finance at your own APR. Then it books the viewing or test drive and sends you the buyer's details. You may see it called an AI chatbot or an AI sales assistant for car dealers; we call it a website sales admin, because that is the job it does.

What happens if the AI gets something wrong?

We don't trust the AI with facts, so it never answers from memory. Everything it says about a car comes from your own stock, and every answer is checked in code before your buyer sees it. A car that isn't in your stock is never offered, and a reserved car is never offered as available. A car's price, mileage, gearbox, fuel and running costs are checked against that car's own listing. Finance figures come from a calculator at your own APR, to the penny, with the full representative example, and any monthly figure that didn't come from it is taken out. You confirm each booking with one tap, and you can read every conversation in your dashboard.

How much does AI live chat cost for a car dealer?

From £299 to £599 a month, depending on how many cars you have in stock. Every plan is the full product: live stock knowledge, test drive booking, finance at your APR, lead capture and your dashboard. Start with a free demo built on your own stock.

Does it work out of hours?

Yes. It answers 24 hours a day, so the buyer browsing at 10pm gets the same answers as one on a Saturday morning. A booking made out of hours goes into your next opening hours, and the lead is waiting for you in the morning.

How do bookings work?

The assistant agrees a specific date and time with the buyer for a viewing or test drive, checks it against your real opening hours (within hours, at least an hour ahead, from half an hour after opening to half an hour before closing), books it, and emails you the booking with the exact car and the customer's details and a button to confirm it.

How do I get the leads?

Every captured lead and every booking lands in your inbox with the car, the customer's name, number and email, and a summary of the chat. Reply to the email and it goes to the buyer. You also get a live dealer dashboard showing conversations, cars discussed and leads with one-tap call and email.

Does it do finance?

It gives a representative finance illustration at your own real representative APR, tied to the specific car the buyer is viewing, never an invented rate, so buyers see a realistic monthly figure and your follow-up starts warm.

Will it work on my website?

Yes, it installs on any dealer website with two lines of code, whatever platform it's built on, and runs in your own branding and name. There is nothing for your team to maintain.

Can it handle a big superstore stock?

Yes. A 1,700-car superstore is no harder for it than a 40-car lot. It holds every car in full detail and finds exact matches in seconds. See our page for superstores.

Is it safe to put in front of my customers?

It is honest by design: it never offers sold or reserved cars, never invents a price, spec or finance rate, and never claims a booking it hasn't made. It speaks as your brand, professionally.

How much does it cost?

From £299 per month, priced to your forecourt size, and every plan is the full product. It starts with a free demo built on your own live stock, so you see it working on your cars before you pay anything.
