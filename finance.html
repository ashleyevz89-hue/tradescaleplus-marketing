Questions

Finance, answered.

Whose finance rate does it use?

Yours. It works out the buyer’s monthly from your own lender’s terms, to the penny, and shows your representative example with it. Where a figure cannot be exact, it says so and offers the nearest it can.

Does it do PCP?

No. It illustrates hire purchase only. PCP goes to your team, with the buyer’s details.

Is it a quote or an illustration?

An illustration. It gives the buyer a realistic monthly figure for the exact car; your team and your finance provider handle the application and the quote, as they do today.

Does it promise approval?

No. Finance is subject to status, and the lender decides.

How do I hear about it?

"Email me this quote" takes the buyer’s name, email and mobile, and you get the quote at the deposit and term they chose.
