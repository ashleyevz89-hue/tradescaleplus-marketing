The superstore problem

Big stock,
big leak.

A big stock list should be your biggest advantage: whatever the buyer wants, you probably have it. On the website, it becomes your biggest leak. The more cars you hold, the more results every search returns, the more pages every buyer must wade through, and the more of them run out of patience before they reach the right car. You paid to bring that buyer to the site. The stock they wanted was there. The website lost the sale in between.

No amount of filter design fixes this, because the problem is not the filters, it is asking buyers to do a database query when what they have is a sentence: “family diesel estate around £12k, automatic.”

The fix

One sentence in, right car out.

The website sales admin takes the sentence and does the work:

  • Knows all of it. Every car, in full detail, spec, features, price, mileage, colour, fuel, gearbox, MOT. A 1,700-car superstore is no harder for it than a 40-car lot, and it relearns the whole stock every day.
  • Matches in seconds. Body type, fuel, gearbox, colour, budget with a sensible stretch band, even specific features, and it takes the buyer straight to the car's page. No scrolling, no filters.
  • Only cars you can sell. Sold and reserved cars never appear in its answers.
  • Every conversation at once. Saturday afternoon surge, bank holiday evening, it holds them all, each one at full knowledge.
  • Then it closes. It books the viewing or test drive at a real time inside your opening hours, quotes finance at your real APR, and captures the lead for your sales team.

Questions, answered

Superstores, answered.

Can it really handle a 1,700-car stock list?

Yes. A 1,700-car superstore is no harder for the assistant than a 40-car lot. It holds every car in full detail, relearns the whole stock every day, and finds exact matches in seconds.

Our website already has good filters. Why add this?

Filters serve the buyer who knows exactly what they want. Everyone else, the “family diesel estate around £12k” buyer, gets 60 results and fatigue. The assistant serves them conversationally and takes them straight to the right car's page, so the size of your site stops being a reason to leave.

How does it handle busy periods and out-of-hours traffic?

Identically. It holds every conversation at once, at any hour, at full knowledge, evenings, weekends and bank holidays included, which is when superstore traffic peaks.

Does it work across our existing website platform?

Yes. It installs on any dealer website with two lines of code, wears your branding, and needs nothing maintained by your team. Your stock feeds it, it relearns every day.

What do our sales team actually receive?

Named, qualified leads with the exact car, contact details and a chat summary, and booked viewings and test drives at real times inside your opening hours, emailed as they happen and listed live in your dealer dashboard.
