Forecourt cards

A code in every windscreen.
Nothing to install.

Your gates are shut fourteen hours a day and people still walk the forecourt. They look at a car, they have a question nobody can answer, and they go home. A card in the window fixes that, and it is the one thing here you can start on your own this afternoon.

The problem

Your best hours are the ones you are shut.

Evenings and Sundays are when people look at cars without a salesperson standing next to them. They wander the front row, find something they like, and then hit the wall: what has it done, when is the MOT, what would it be a month, is it still here. Nobody is in. The sticker in the screen does not say. So they take a photo, mean to ring on Monday, and never do.

That buyer was as close to buying as anyone gets. They were stood next to the car.

How it works

Print them once.
That is the install.

We give you a sheet with a card for every car on your forecourt, ready to print on ordinary A4. Each one carries that car's own code. You cut them out and put them in the windscreens, and you are live. There is nothing to add to your website, no plugin, no developer, and nobody to ask.

When your stock changes you print the new ones. That is the whole maintenance.

What the buyer gets

A straight answer
about that exact car.

They point their phone at the card and they are talking to something that knows that vehicle: the mileage, the colour, the gearbox, the fuel, the MOT, the price, and the monthly figure at your own representative APR, never an invented one. They can ask what it is like on fuel, whether it will take a dog crate, what else you have like it if this one is too dear.

It does not guess. If it does not know something, it says so and takes their details so you can answer properly.

While you are open

And a button for
“bring the keys out”.

During the day it does something a website cannot. Someone stood by a car taps the button on their screen and your sales floor is told that there is a person outside, at that car, right now. No walking the rows looking for them, no missed customer while everyone is busy with somebody else.

Why this one is different

You do not need
anyone's permission.

Most things you get sold need putting on your website, which means a conversation with whoever built it, a wait, and sometimes a no. The cards need none of that. You print them, and every enquiry that comes off them lands with you in the same place as the rest: a name, a number and the car they were stood next to.

It is the smallest possible way to find out whether this works on your forecourt. If it does nothing, you have lost a sheet of paper.

The rest of it

The same brain,
everywhere else too.

The thing answering on the card is the same one that can answer on your website, where it takes buyers straight to the right car instead of into a filter menu, knows every car you have, and hands you every lead. Your own sales team can ask it too: what have we got in an automatic diesel estate under twelve grand, answered before they have logged into anything.

Have a look at it on your own stock and see what it knows.
