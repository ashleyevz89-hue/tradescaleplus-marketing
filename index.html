How it installs

Two lines of code

Line one is a link tag. Line two is a script. Add both to your website. Nothing else on your site changes.

Your two lines
<link rel="mcp" href="https://demo.tradescaleplus.com/connector/YOUR-SLUG"><noscript><a href="https://demo.tradescaleplus.com/ai/YOUR-SLUG">Our live listings, with prices and a search, readable without JavaScript</a></noscript><script src="https://demo.tradescaleplus.com/embed.js?slug=YOUR-SLUG" data-slug="YOUR-SLUG" async></script>

YOUR-SLUG stands for your own site's name.

Questions, answered

AI agents, answered.

What is an AI agent for a website?

It is software that sits on your website, answers visitors from your own website and data, and does the next job for them: it takes them to the right page, filters the list, compares, saves and books. A chatbot only talks.

How does it get onto my website?

With two lines of code: a link tag and a script. Nothing else on your site changes.

Can it make things up?

Every reply is checked in code before it is shown. Invented prices, invented cars, wrong links and false claims that something is free are caught and corrected. It answers only from your own website and data. We say checked, not perfect.

Who is it for?

Car dealers and property agencies first, and we can also build agents for other websites, such as courses, events, services and catalogues.

Can ChatGPT, Claude and other AI assistants use it?

On a test page, our two install lines led ChatGPT to the live stock on its own, and Claude's reader loaded the agent itself. It is plain web pages, so any AI that reads the web can use it. There is also an MCP connector for AI apps that take connectors. No one can promise what an AI will recommend, because AI assistants choose their own answers.

What does it cost?

Every agent is priced for your business. The price depends on how many cars or listings you have, how many websites or branches the agent covers and the features you use. Get a quote.
