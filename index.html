Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions.

What does TradeScale Plus actually build?

We build AI systems that run the customer side of a business: an AI receptionist that answers your calls, an AI that reads your messages and keeps your CRM up to date, and an automation engine that drafts your follow-ups, review requests, reminders, campaigns and documents. We also build smart websites, custom software and bespoke AI agents for any part of a company's operations.

How does the AI learn my business?

We point it at your website. It builds a structured knowledge base of your actual services, products, stock and prices, so it answers customers like someone who works there rather than from a generic script. For a car dealer that means knowing every car on the forecourt; for a clinic, every treatment.

Do the AI's replies send automatically?

No, and that is deliberate. The AI does the admin on its own: it books appointments, moves your pipeline and captures contact details autonomously. But every message a customer sees is drafted for you first, in your voice, and sent with your one-tap approval. You keep control of every word.

Can it handle WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram messages?

Yes. The inbox is built for phone calls, SMS, WhatsApp, web chat, email and Facebook and Instagram DMs. Calls, SMS, WhatsApp and web chat run as soon as your numbers are connected, and email and social channels are configured for your accounts during onboarding.

Is this just a chatbot?

No. A chatbot answers questions on one channel. This is a full operating system: a voice AI on your phone line, a text AI across your message channels, a CRM and pipeline that maintain themselves, an automation engine for the follow-up work, and an AI advisor for the owner. And because we are a studio rather than a boxed product, we build whatever else your business needs on top.

How much does it cost?

Every build is bespoke, so we price to the job. You get a clear price after a free consultation, with no obligation. Most clients start with one build that fixes their biggest leak, then a simple monthly plan to keep the system running and improving.

How fast can a system go live?

A first working system is usually live within one to two weeks. Larger builds ship in stages, so you see progress from the first week instead of waiting months for a big reveal.

Who is TradeScale Plus for, and where are you based?

We are based in South Wales and work with businesses across the UK. The platform fits any enquiry-led or booking-led business: car dealers, estate and letting agents, trades, home improvement, health and beauty, hospitality and professional services. If you take enquiries and turn them into booked work, it fits, and if you need something bespoke, we build it.
