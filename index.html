Questions

The Website Agent, answered.

What is the Website Agent?

It is the part of your website that talks to buyers. It works inside your own pages: a search bar on your home page and stock list that drives the list to the cars a buyer describes in their own words, and a panel on every car page with their monthly at your own rate, a side by side, part-exchange details for your team and test drive booking inside your opening hours. It answers only from your own stock and pages.

Do I need a new website?

No. Two lines of code go on the website you already have, added by your web company or through your own Google Tag Manager. We set it up and run it.

Can it make things up?

It answers only from your own stock and pages, and every reply is checked in code before a buyer sees it, against your own stock and figures. When only your team can answer, it says so and takes the buyer's details.

Where do the leads go?

Every enquiry and test drive request is emailed to you the moment it is made, with the car, the buyer's name and number and what they want. Reply and it goes straight to the buyer. Every conversation is also kept in your dashboard.

Is it for estate agents too?

Yes, for UK and Dubai estate agents: a search that counts your homes as a buyer types, and a panel on every listing with viewings booked for the listing's consultant, homes like it and what is nearby. For estate agents

What does it cost?

It is priced for your business: how many cars or listings you have, how many websites or branches it covers and the features you use. Get a quote
