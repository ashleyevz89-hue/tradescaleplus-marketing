Where the answers come from

Your website. Nothing else.

It answers only from your own stock and pages: each car’s own listing, your opening hours, your finance and what your website says.

It reads your website every day: new cars in, sold cars out, prices as you set them.

Every reply is checked in code before a buyer sees it, against your own stock and figures. When only your team can answer, it says so and takes the buyer’s details.
