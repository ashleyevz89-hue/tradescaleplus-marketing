Not a script

Car-by-car, in full detail.

Most car dealership website chatbots know nothing about the cars. They answer off a FAQ, take a message, and leave the buyer exactly where they started. This one is different because it starts from your stock, not from a script.

Every car on your forecourt sits in the assistant's knowledge in full detail:

  • Make, model, year and trim, so it knows a 2021 A3 S line from a 2019 A3 Sport.
  • Price and mileage, so “cheapest automatic under £15k?” gets a real, correct answer.
  • Colour, fuel, gearbox and body type, so it can match what a buyer actually asked for.
  • Spec and features: parking sensors, heated seats, sat nav, towbar. If the car has it, the assistant knows.
  • MOT: asked about it, it answers straight.

And because it relearns your stock every day, it is never more than a day behind your forecourt: a car you sell drops out at the next daily refresh, and a car you take in appears.

Why it matters

Answers sell cars.
Silence loses them.

When a buyer has a question about a specific car and no one is there to answer it, most of them do not ring you in the morning, they keep scrolling and buy from whoever answered. The Car Agent answers on the spot, at 11pm on a Sunday as readily as 11am on a Tuesday, and then does what a good salesperson does next: it moves the buyer towards the car, books the viewing or test drive, and captures the lead.

It is also honest by design. It never offers a sold or reserved car, never invents a price or a spec, and never claims a booking it has not made. It is your brand talking, professionally.

Questions, answered

Live stock, answered.

How does the assistant know my stock?

It reads your actual live stock from your website, car by car: make, model, year, price, mileage, colour, fuel, gearbox, spec, features and MOT. It relearns your stock every day.

What happens when a car sells?

It drops out of the assistant's answers. It only ever offers cars that are actually available, it never recommends a sold or reserved car.

Can it really answer detailed questions about a specific car?

Yes. Ask it whether the black Q2 has parking sensors, what the cheapest automatic under £15,000 is, or how many 7-seaters you have in stock, and it answers instantly and accurately from your real stock data.

Does it work for a big forecourt?

That is the problem it is built for. On a big stock list buyers get lost in filters. The agent answers from your own stock, refreshed daily from your own site, and takes the buyer to the right car.

Will it ever make something up about a car?

No. It never invents a price, a spec or a finance rate. If it does not know, it says so and offers to book the buyer in with your team instead.
