How a lead is made

Four things a buyer does instead of leaving.

A test drive, to confirm

The day, the time inside your opening hours, the car and the buyer’s number. Press Confirm and a buyer who gave their email gets their confirmation straight away, and a reminder the day before.

A finance quote, to send

"Email me this quote" takes their name, email and mobile, and you get the quote at the deposit and term they chose.

A part-exchange, to value

Their registration and mileage, for your team to come back to them with a figure. No price is given.

A question for your team

When only your team can answer, it says so and takes the buyer’s details.

Questions

Your leads, answered.

Where do the leads go?

Every enquiry and test drive request is emailed to you the moment it is made, with the car, the buyer’s name and number and what they want. Reply and it goes straight to the buyer. Every conversation is also kept in your dashboard.

What does HOT mean?

A buyer who is ready to go gets HOT in the subject line, so you know who to ring first.

What about leads after we close?

They are emailed to you just the same, and counted apart in your dashboard, so you see what the evenings are worth.

Does it value part-exchanges?

No. It takes the buyer’s registration and mileage and sends them to your team. No price is given.

What if it cannot answer a buyer?

When only your team can answer, it says so and takes the buyer’s details for you.
