Questions, answered

Pricing, answered.

How much does the Car Agent cost?

From £299 up to £599 a month, depending on your stock size. You get your exact figure with your demo.

How much do the Property Agent and the Custom Website Agent cost?

Pricing is on request. Book a demo and we will talk it through.

Is there a free trial?

For car dealers, you can start with a free demo built on your own stock.

What does the monthly price include?

For car dealers: the agent on your website, answering from your own stock, taking visitors to the right car, booking viewings and test drives within your opening hours, and quoting finance at your own published rate. Plus enquiries by email, hot-lead alerts, a dashboard and a Monday report.

How long does installation take?

It installs with two lines of code: a link tag and a script. Nothing else on your site changes.

Why is the demo free for car dealers?

Because the agent sells itself best on your own stock. Once you have asked it about your own cars, the decision is easy either way.
