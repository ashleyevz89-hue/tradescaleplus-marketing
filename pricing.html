Questions, answered

Pricing, answered.

How much does the website sales admin cost?

From £299 per month, priced to your forecourt size. A 40-car lot and a 1,700-car superstore are different jobs, so the price scales with the stock, you get your exact figure with your free demo.

Is there a free trial?

Better: a free demo built on your own live stock. You see the assistant answering questions about your actual cars, on a demo of your site, before you decide anything.

What does the monthly price include?

The website sales admin live on your website, in your branding, relearning your stock every day, answering, navigating, booking viewings and test drives, quoting finance at your APR, capturing leads, plus the booking and lead emails and your dealer dashboard login.

How long does installation take?

It installs with two lines of code on any dealer website. Your web person pastes the snippet; there is nothing for your team to maintain.

Because the product sells itself best on your own stock. Once you have asked it about your own cars and watched it book a viewing, the decision is easy either way.
