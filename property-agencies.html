Questions, answered

The Property Agent, answered.

What does the agent do on a property website?

It lists, compares and saves listings, and books viewings or takes the enquiry. It also takes the visitor to the right page and filters your listings page.

Can it convert prices between currencies?

Yes. It converts prices at that day's published reference rates, for example AED to USD.

Can it make up a price or a link?

Every reply is checked in code before it is shown. Invented prices, wrong links and false claims that something is free are caught and corrected. It answers only from your own website and data.

Is there a live demo?

Yes: TradeScale Property, a sample Dubai agency with invented listings. Try it live: search the homes, ask the agent about any of them, compare them and see prices in US dollars.

Can ChatGPT read my listings?

On a test page of car listings, drawn by JavaScript, our two install lines led ChatGPT to the live stock on its own. The install is the same two lines on any website. No one can promise what an AI will recommend, because AI assistants choose their own answers.

What does it cost?

It is priced for your business. The price depends on how many listings you have, how many websites or branches the agent covers and the features you use. Get a quote.
