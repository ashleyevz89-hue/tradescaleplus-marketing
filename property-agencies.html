01 The interactive website

Your listings, answering for themselves.

Two places on your website change. Your home and search pages get the bar. Every listing gets the panel, under its price. Nothing else on your site is rebuilt.

The bar, on your home and search pages

It counts your homes as they type.

Before a word, it shows how many homes you list to buy and to rent. As the visitor types, it counts the homes that fit, says what it understood, and opens your own search page for exactly those. A question goes to the conversation, right there in the bar.

Questions

For estate agents, answered.

Is it for UK agencies as well as Dubai?

Yes. The Website Agent works for estate agents in the UK and in Dubai, on the website you already have.

How are viewings booked?

The buyer or tenant picks one of the next days a viewing can be asked for, and a morning, afternoon or evening. It goes to the listing's consultant as a request to confirm with them.

What does it answer from?

Only your own listings and pages. Every reply is checked in code before a buyer sees it. When only your team can answer, it says so and takes the buyer's details.

Do I need a new website?

No. Two lines of code go on the website you already have, added by your web company or through your own Google Tag Manager. We set it up and run it.

What does it cost?

It is priced for your business: how many listings you have, how many websites or branches it covers and the features you use. Get a quote
