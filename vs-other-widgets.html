Where each one fits

Three alternatives, plainly.

Live chat. Great whenever someone is free to answer it, and the Website Agent works happily alongside it. But a person can only answer as fast as they can look the car up, and out of hours, when most buyers browse, live chat can only take a message.

The generic AI chatbot. Bolted on, trained on a FAQ, and blind to the forecourt. It cannot take a buyer to a car it does not know exists, cannot book a real slot, and cannot quote finance on anything.

The lead form. A letterbox. It answers nothing, shows nothing and books nothing.

The Website Agent does the whole job inside your own pages, at every hour: it answers from each car’s own listing, drives your stock list to the right cars, books test drives inside your opening hours, works out finance at your own APR, and sends you the lead.

Questions

The comparison, answered.

How is this different from a chat bubble?

A chat bubble is a button in the corner, laid over your page. The Website Agent works inside your own pages: a bar in your search area that drives your stock list, and a panel under every car with the buyer’s tools.

How is this different from live chat?

Live chat is great when someone is there to answer it, and the two work side by side. The difference is every hour of the day: the Website Agent answers from your own stock, books the test drive and works out finance on the actual car, day and night.

How is this different from a generic AI chatbot?

A generic chatbot answers off a FAQ. The Website Agent answers only from your own stock and pages, reads your website every day, and every reply is checked in code before a buyer sees it.

Aren’t lead forms enough?

A lead form is a one-way letterbox. It answers no questions, shows no cars and books nothing. The Website Agent does those on the page and sends you the lead, with the car named.

Does it replace my staff?

No, it feeds them. It works the website day and night and sends your team every lead with the car and the buyer’s details. Your people do what they are best at: selling cars to people who turn up.
