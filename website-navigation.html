Where it is

In your page. Not in a corner.

The Website Agent isn’t a chat bubble in the corner. It works inside your pages, in the places buyers already look: your search, and every car.

On your home page and stock list that means the bar. It sits in your page at one size, and never covers it. Your filters stay where they are: they work for the buyer who knows exactly what they want. The bar is for the buyer who would rather just say it.

On a phone the bar stacks under your search, in one column, with the question box always in reach.

Questions

The search bar, answered.

How does a buyer use it?

They type what they want in their own words into the bar on your home page or stock list. Your own stock list then shows exactly those cars.

Does it ever show sold or reserved cars?

No. It counts and shows cars from your live stock, never a sold or reserved one.

Does it replace my website’s filters?

No. Your filters stay where they are. They work for buyers who know exactly what they want; the bar is for buyers who would rather say it in their own words.

What if a buyer types a question, not a search?

A question is answered right there in the bar, from your own stock and pages.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. On a phone the bar stacks under your search in one column, and it never covers your page.
